RETAIL INVENTORY & REVENUE ANALYTICS — DATASET NOTES

This is synthetic sample data created for a portfolio learning project. It does not represent a real company.
Reporting period: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Currency: INR.
Sales lines: 670; unique orders: 420; products: 30; stores: 6; inventory rows: 180.

Files:
- Retail_Inventory_Revenue_Dataset.xlsx: master workbook with Overview, Sales, Products, Stores, Inventory, and Data_Dictionary sheets.
- CSV/Sales.csv, Products.csv, Stores.csv, Inventory.csv: separate flat files for database loading.

Relationships:
- Sales.Product_ID -> Products.Product_ID
- Sales.Store_ID -> Stores.Store_ID
- Inventory.Product_ID -> Products.Product_ID
- Inventory.Store_ID -> Stores.Store_ID

Inventory consistency: Closing_Stock = Opening_Stock + Stock_Received - Units_Sold_2025.
Units_Sold_2025 is reconciled to Sales by Product_ID and Store_ID.
